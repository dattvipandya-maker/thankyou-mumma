My dearest Mumma,

Kya lakhu tara mate? Kyathi start karu? Ketli badhi vaat che je tane kehvi che, pan words hamesha ochha padi jay che.

Jyaare hu pain ma hati, tu mari strength hati. Jyaare hu hospital ma hati, tu mari pase hati. Jyaare tu ghare hati ane hu hospital ma ekli hati, tara phone calls e mane kyarey ekli feel na thava didhi.

I hope hu tane proud feel karavi shaku. I hope hu tane e badhi happiness aapi shaku je tu deserve kare che.

Thank you for choosing my happiness even on the days when you were tired. Thank you for being my mother, my strength and my forever home.

You gave me life once when I was born.
You gave me life again on 1st September.

I love you more than words will ever be able to say.

Forever your little girl,
